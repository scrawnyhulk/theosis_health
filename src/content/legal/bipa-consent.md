**Biometric consent (required for photo features)**

To draw your figure and create AI illustrations, we estimate body-shape landmarks from your photo, and images of your face may be processed. Illinois and some other states treat this as **biometric information**.

- **Purpose:** only to make the figure and illustrations you request. No facial recognition or identification.
- **Where it goes:** it's processed on your device. For AI illustrations, your photo is sent to our processor, Google (Gemini API). We don't store it on our servers.
- **How long:** saved photos stay on your device until you delete them. Everything is destroyed when the purpose is done, or within 3 years of your last use at most.
- **Never sold.** We never sell or profit from it.

Full details: [Privacy Policy, Section 3](/privacy#biometric).
