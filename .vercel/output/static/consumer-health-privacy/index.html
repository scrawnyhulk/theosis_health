Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — Theosis Health

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada, and other states with consumer health data laws. Theosis Health LLC, 4026 Bristol Dr., Danville, IL 61834, support@theosishealth.com.

  • What health data we collect: None on our servers. Health-related inputs you enter in our tools (such as weight, waist, diabetes status, A1c, or glucose) are stored only in your browser on your device. If you choose to include health details in a contact-form message or email, we receive that message. If you turn on optional AI features, what you type or the food photo you choose (which may include health details) is sent to Google Gemini (paid API) to answer that request, with your consent, and is not stored by us. Google acts as our service provider and may keep it briefly for abuse monitoring under its terms.
  • Sources: only you.
  • Why: to run the tools on your device and to reply to your message. Nothing else.
  • Who we share it with: only our service providers that deliver messages for us (Netlify Forms and Microsoft 365 through GoDaddy). Google (Gemini API), as our service provider, only for AI requests you choose to make after giving consent. We don't sell consumer health data, share it with third parties for their own use, or use it for advertising.
  • Your rights: you can ask to confirm whether we have your health data, see it, get a list of who received it, delete it, or withdraw consent. Data on your device you can delete yourself by clearing your browser's site data for theosishealth.com. Email support@theosishealth.com. We'll respond within 45 days. If we deny your request, reply "Appeal." If you're still unhappy, you can contact your state Attorney General.
  • No geofencing around health-care locations.

For everything else, see our Privacy Policy.
