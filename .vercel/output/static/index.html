Welcome to Theosis Health

We’ve all heard the advice: eat less, move more. Daily life makes it more complicated than that. Most of us have a general sense of what a “healthy diet” looks like, but putting it into practice day to day can be hard. Small food choices that don’t seem significant can add up more than we realize, and research shows metabolism can start drifting off track years before a diagnosis of type 2 diabetes.1

If the human body were a machine, protein would be the parts, and carbohydrates and fats would be the fuel. Genetics help set how much fuel each person can safely store. Once that capacity is exceeded, fat tends to build up in organs like the liver and pancreas, which can contribute to insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes.2 These changes often develop quietly, before obvious symptoms appear. See what happens inside as fat builds up →

Theosis Health helps you look under the hood: how the human body works, what can knock it off track, and where simple changes can help. We pair clear explanations with practical tools, so you can focus on manageable choices that make a meaningful difference. It starts with knowing what matters and making it work in real life.

Below are a couple of simple choices that can quietly derail our health, or be low-hanging fruit that moves things in the right direction, even if everything else stays the same.

  • 40.1 million people in the US have diabetes (2023)3
  • 115.2 million US adults have prediabetes3
  • About 1 in 3 US adults (34.7%) have metabolic syndrome (2011–2016)4
