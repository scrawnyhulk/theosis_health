Welcome to Theosis Health

Our bodies are a lot like machines. We’re made of parts (protein) and run on fuel (carbohydrates and fats). Most adults burn roughly 60–110 calories an hour, so a few sips of a sweet drink or a few extra bites can be more than we need in that moment. We store the extra, and small things add up fast.

We each have a different-sized fuel tank for storing fat safely, set largely by our genes. Once ours is full, the extra fuel has nowhere safe to go. It gets harder to clear sugar and fat from the bloodstream, so blood sugar starts to rise. That tipping point is our personal fat threshold, and it’s how so many people slide into insulin resistance, prediabetes and type 2 diabetes without realizing it. See what happens inside as fat builds up → Today 40.1 million people in the US have diabetes, 115.2 million adults have prediabetes,1 and about 1 in 3 adults have metabolic syndrome.2

Theosis Health and the Theosis Metabolic Simulator were created to help you find small, targeted changes that fit the life you already have, without giving up every food you enjoy.
