Welcome to Theosis Health

Growing up as a scrawny yet nerdy kid who wanted to put on muscle, I was told five hundred different recommendations about five hundred different ways to lift and eat, except it just never seemed to work right for me.

Nowadays there are plenty of health influencers and diet programs, each with their own take on health and fitness. And although there’s a lot of overlap, I haven’t really seen much that addresses the pathology of poor health. In our current food landscape it’s incredibly easy to overconsume calories every day, and that’s what leads to metabolic strain and insulin resistance.

So it’s become incredibly important to me to find a way to reach everybody and help them understand how food-related diseases occur, so we can work together to get back to better health — one person at a time.

  • 40.1 million people in the US have diabetes (2023)1
  • 115.2 million US adults have prediabetes1
  • About 1 in 3 US adults (34.7%) have metabolic syndrome (2011–2016)2
  1. CDC. National Diabetes Statistics Report (updated Sept 16, 2026).
  2. Hirode G, Wong RJ. Trends in the prevalence of metabolic syndrome in the United States, 2011–2016. JAMA. 2020;323(24):2526–2528.
